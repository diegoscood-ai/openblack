How a computer god uses miracles

A computer god casts the miracles its towns and temple give it, as a person does: it picks the best miracle of a kind for the job, makes sure it has the prayer power, charges the icon if needed and casts it at the right place. The land scripts also cast miracles in a god's name, from where its hand is, for staged attacks.

Progress: 3/38 done, 9 partial — 20%

See ../miracles/ for each miracle, and ai.md for when a god wants to cast.
