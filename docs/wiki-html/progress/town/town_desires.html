Town desires

Each town works out, every game turn, how much it wants each of seventeen things: food, wood, playtime, protection, mercy, abodes, civic buildings, worship supplies, children, building, rain, sun, repairs, workshop supplies, a wonder, relaxation and sleep. Idle villagers serve the most wanted. The storage pit shows the town's wants as flags.

Progress: 19/26 done, 2 partial — 77%
