Creature gestures

Gestures that involve the creature beyond the leash: the player's gestures during a creature fight, and the gestures the creature itself draws in the air when it casts a miracle. The creature's emotes (blowing a kiss, waving and so on) are creature actions, in ../creature/; the leash gestures are in leash_gestures.md.
