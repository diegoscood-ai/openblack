Placement

Where the god hand sits in the world, how large it is drawn and which way it faces. The game keeps the hand under the cursor at all times, hanging over the land, the sea, the object or the temple wall the cursor points at.

Progress: 17/29 done, 6 partial — 69%

How the original does it, in our wiki: The hand and the interface.
