Gold scrolls

The gold scrolls are the story: the quests the player must finish to move through the lands. Each one is started by its land's control script and logged in the story's challenge log. The optional quests are the silver scrolls (silver_scrolls.md).

This is an index, not a scored file. Each quest's own file in gold_scrolls/ holds its rows; until a quest has one, its only coverage is the single summary row in its land file.

Where things stand (2026-10-08)

  • No gold quest runs to its end in openblack yet. Land 1's control script stops at Choose Your Creature's gate stones (ObjectInfoBits is a stub) unless the creature training is skipped, no land's exit loads the next land and the control scripts of Lands 2 to 5 never run (LOAD_MAP does nothing), and some commands the quests need (rewards, creature control, the challenge log) are still stubs in src/CHLApi.cpp. See silver_scrolls.md for the blockers both kinds share.
  • Every gold quest now has its own file in gold_scrolls/ (32 files, cut quests included), plus creature_guide.md for the guide's part of Land 1 and tutorial_island.md for the tutorial's six lessons. They score 0–53%: no quest is done.
  • Losing (losing_and_game_over.md) and the land-to-land vortex (portals.md) have their own files. Bugs in the original quests are collected in ../vanilla_bugs/.

Coverage: summary = only the land file's one row; file = its own quest file.
