Leash gestures

The player puts the leash on their creature and picks which leash it wears by drawing gestures. What each leash does to the creature is in ../creature/leash.md.

Progress: 0/15 done, 0 partial — 0%

How the original does it, in our wiki: Magic: the core of the miracles.

A scribble takes the leash off or closes the picker: see scribble.md.
