Desires

A creature is driven by about forty desires (hunger, anger, compassion, the wish to play, to sleep, to be friends …). Each desire grows from its sources (low energy, darkness, the player's strokes, its own innate character …) once they pass their thresholds, fades otherwise, and the strongest desires decide what the creature does and what it shows the player.
