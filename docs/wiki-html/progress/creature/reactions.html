Reactions

A creature reacts to what happens around it: miracles cast near it, fires, deaths, fights, things flying past, food, balls, teleport stones, the player's hand at work, things crushed and things that hit it, and other creatures. Each kind of reaction has a priority that decides whether it drops what it is doing, and a time before it reacts to the same kind again.
