Splitting and heating rocks

Rocks and boulders can be broken in two by the hand, by the creature and by hard knocks, again and again, each half a smaller rock of the same stone. They can also be heated: stone never burns away, so a rock stays as hot as whatever heated it, glows, sets things alight around it and cools slowly. This file goes into the detail behind the rock rows of rocks_and_features.md, ../hand/clicking_and_activating.md, ../physics/collisions.md and ../physics/fire.md; the fire model itself (heat, spreading, cooling, water) is in the fire file.

openblack: src/ECS/Rocks.cpp (splitting and the tap), src/ECS/Physics/PhysicsObjects.cpp (the knocks), src/Fire/FireModel.cpp and src/ECS/Fire/FireEffect.cpp (heat). The creature's blow is not ported.

How a rock splits

Behaviour Status Where / notes
The rock is replaced by two rocks of the same kind, each 0.7935 of its scale (about half its volume, so half its weight), turned to its heading, at full life done Rocks::SplitInTwo (the Y angle kept, new rocks at full life)
The halves lie either side of where it was, along a line at a random angle (from the game's random numbers), each 0.7935 of its width from the centre, at its height done Rocks::SplitInTwo (a synced random angle, 0.7935 of its 2D radius, its altitude)
A flying rock's halves fly on at its speed with half its spin; a resting rock's halves start still and drop done ReactToPhysicsImpact in src/ECS/Physics/PhysicsObjects.cpp passes them to Rocks::SplitInTwo
A flying rock the physics has lost track of doesn't split done Only a rock in the physics list takes the impact
A burning or hot rock's halves both carry its heat done fire::CopyFire from Rocks::SplitInTwo; see ../physics/fire.md
The halves keep the rock's owner (the player who last threw it) todo Rocks keep no owner in our tree
The halves are new rocks: if the rock was a town's artefact, that is lost with it todo No town artefacts in our tree; see ../town/artefacts.md
Nothing limits how often a rock can be split; only the hand's tap and knocks stop at rocks 0.7 m tall or less done Rocks::ValidToTap and the wear test both need a height over 0.7
A split from a knock has no dust, particles or sound of its own; only the tap and the creature's blow play a sound done ReactToPhysicsImpact plays nothing; Rocks::Tap plays the tap's sound

Knocks and landings

Behaviour Status Where / notes
Every turn, every rock in the physics that took a blow (from what it hit, what hit it, or the ground) is worn by it done EndTurn and ReactToPhysicsImpact in src/ECS/Physics/PhysicsObjects.cpp
A blow wears a rock only when it is harder than four times the rock's weight and the rock is taller than 0.7 m done ReactToPhysicsImpact (G over 4, height over 0.7)
A rock is never worn by another rock, so rocks thrown at rocks bounce off whole done ReactToPhysicsImpact (not when the hitter is a rock)
Each such blow takes (strength over its weight − 4) × 0.005 of its life; below 0.01 life it splits done ReactToPhysicsImpact ((G - 4) x 0.005; below 0.01 it splits)
A rock thrown hard into the ground or a building can wear and split itself on landing done Every body's blow is measured (EndTurn)
Miracles and everything else split rocks only through such blows, when something throws a rock about; no miracle, script or other rule splits a rock directly done Only the knock and the hand's tap split rocks in our tree

Heating

Behaviour Status Where / notes
Every rock and boulder burns at 500 degrees, holds heat like 4000 units, and is never hurt by fire done Read from info.dat (src/ECS/Fire/FireObjectTraits.cpp)
Stone never heats itself: even at 500 or more a rock does not burn hotter, it only cools whenever nothing heated it that turn done fire::Step (src/Fire/FireModel.cpp): an object with no burn defence only cools
A rock gets only as hot as what heats it: burning trees (at most 220 degrees) and huts (300) can't bring it to 500; the fireball (up to 6000), lightning and scripts can done The model's fire::HeatTransfer (src/Fire/FireModel.cpp, the heat capped by the difference)
A fireball's ball and its burn heat a rock like any other object done src/Magic/Objects/MagicFireBall.cpp, fire::ApplyEffectToFireEffectIfNecessary (over fire::ApplyBurn, src/Fire/FireModel.cpp)
A rock held in the hand is heated by the fires in its cell, but only while the hand is inside its player's influence done fire::CheckToSeeIfObjectIsNearOnFireObject from HandTurn.cpp, inside the holder's influence
A hot rock held in the hand passes its heat on only inside the holder's influence done The spread of a held object in src/ECS/Fire/FireEffect.cpp (only inside the holder's influence)
The hand is never hurt by a hot rock and never drops it done Nothing ties the hand to heat
From 400 degrees flames grow on a rock, full at 1000; at 500 or more it is on fire done fire::FireFraction (src/Fire/FireModel.cpp), src/ECS/Fire/FireGraphic.cpp
Below 500 a hot rock heats only things that catch at or below its temperature (villagers 120, trees 110, huts 150, fences 300) and loses the heat it gives; at 500 or more it heats everything within reach without losing any done The model's fire::HeatTransfer (src/Fire/FireModel.cpp) and the spread (src/ECS/Fire/FireEffect.cpp)
A thrown hot rock sets alight what it lands among by the same rules, its reach being its fire's radius plus 10 m done Flying things are off the map for fire; once at rest it spreads again (src/ECS/Fire/FireEffect.cpp); see ../physics/fire.md
Hotter than 100 degrees a rock makes villagers within 35 m react as to a fire done The fire's reaction (src/ECS/Fire/FireEffect.cpp)
Villagers flee a burning rock carried in the hand partial The reaction is made, but villagers have no handler for it; see ../physics/fire.md
In the open a rock cools by its surface over its 4000 units of heat, so a big rock stays hot for minutes; rain cools it faster done fire::Step and fire::CoolingArea (src/Fire/FireModel.cpp)
Dropped in water (or on the shore, under 2 m up) it cools fifty times faster, hissing with steam once while hotter than 75 partial Fifty times faster (fire::Step, src/Fire/FireModel.cpp) and the steam above 75 (src/ECS/Fire/FireGraphic.cpp); no hiss sound in our tree
A hot rock glows red-orange, a thousandth of full per degree with a 20% flicker, full at 1000 done graphic::CharringGlow (src/ECS/Fire/FireGraphic.cpp; the flicker's noise is approximated)
A burning rock crackles like any fire, the nearest fire on the ground holding the loop done src/ECS/Fire/FireSound.cpp. Our wiki differs: the two fires nearest the camera hold the loop, not one (magic.md)
A script can ask whether something is on fire near a place; a rock at 500 or more counts done script::IsFireNear (src/Magic/Script/CHLFire.cpp)
