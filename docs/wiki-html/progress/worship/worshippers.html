Worshippers and dancing

The villagers who leave their town to dance at the worship site. The player sets how many of a town worship; the dancers chant prayer power, tire, get hungry and can die of it.

Progress: 10/23 done, 5 partial — 54%

How the original does it, in our wiki: Magic: the core of the miracles.
