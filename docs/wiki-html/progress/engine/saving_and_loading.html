Saving and loading

Saving the whole game to a slot and loading it back: the world with every object in it, the players and their creature, the towns, the scripts, the camera, the weather and the temple. Saves live in the player's profile and are made from the temple's save game room, by the quick save keys, by scripts, and by an autosave. openblack has no save-game system yet.
