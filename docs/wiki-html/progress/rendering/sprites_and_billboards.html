Sprites and billboards

Flat textured pictures drawn in the world rather than models: camera-facing sprites, sprites lying in their own plane, the glows of lights and lanterns, and text laid out in the world. Particle sprites are in particles.md; the moon and sun in ../sky/; the influence ring in ../worship/.
