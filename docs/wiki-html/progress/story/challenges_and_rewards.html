Challenges and rewards

How a challenge runs across the lands: it is found by its scroll, recorded when started and finished with how well it went and how good or evil the player was, and pays out a reward. The scrolls themselves are in ../interface/scrolls_and_signs.md.

Every silver scroll, land by land, is listed in silver_scrolls.md; the shared creature swap is in silver_scrolls/creature_swaps.md and the breeder in silver_scrolls/the_creature_breeder.md.
