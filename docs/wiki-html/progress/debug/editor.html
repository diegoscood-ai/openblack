Editor

openblack's in-game editor, toggled with F2: a tool bar, an outliner, an inspector and tabs for the palette and the scripts' log, round the view of the world, where the mouse picks, moves, turns and places things. The Log tab and the debug bar's Scripts window are in script_debugger.md.

Progress: 25/32 done, 1 partial — 80%

How the original does it, in our wiki: openblack internals.
