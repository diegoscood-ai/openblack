Wood miracle

Held at the side of the hand like the horn, the wood miracle pours logs for four seconds; they land as wood piles or go into a storage pit, a workshop or a building site. There is no extreme version. Wood used for building is in ../resources/.

Progress: 17/23 done, 4 partial — 83%
