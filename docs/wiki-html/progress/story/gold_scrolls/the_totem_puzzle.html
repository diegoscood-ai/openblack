The Totem Puzzle

The gold scroll on the fourth land that frees the first of Nemesis's Guardian Stones, the one sealed under a spiritual shield beside the Japanese village, which calls down the fireballs. Five bell towers (totems) stand round the stone; the village elder asks the player to work out their secret. It is a "follow the bells" memory game: the towers ring a sequence and the player clicks them back in the same order, four rounds of growing length, with the time allowed to click shrinking each round.

Land: 4 · Giver: the Japanese village's elder, at the bell towers by the village · Script: Land4Puzzle (in Land4Meteorites) · Reward: the fire Guardian Stone is destroyed and the fireballs stop for good · Repeatable: retried until solved (each failure needs the scroll clicked again); not after

The land as a whole is in ../land_4.md; the other stones are freed by The Heartbroken Man and The Defending Ogres. The two raised totems that cure the skeletons belong to Undead Village, a different puzzle.

Sources: the land's challenge scripts (the original source text, which matches the shipped challenge.chl) and the game's text table (Scripts/InfoScript2.txt) for every line and its voice. openblack's state is judged on this tree: the story's top script always runs the first land's control script first, the map-loading command (LoadMap in src/CHLApi.cpp) is empty and the first land stalls long before its end, so this quest never runs. Rows are partial only where every command they need works in openblack.
