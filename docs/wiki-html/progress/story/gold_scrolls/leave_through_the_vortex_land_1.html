Leave Through the Vortex (Land 1)

The first land's last gold scroll: after Nemesis's storm kills the guide, a golden light marks a vortex that opens near the coast, swallows a shepherd and throws him back out, and the advisors tell the player to send people and supplies through and then follow. Clicking the scroll over the vortex dives the camera into it and ends the land. The quest's flow is here; how vortices work (what they suck in, what crosses, the arrival) is in ../portals.md.

Land: 1 · Giver: the two advisors, at the vortex near the coast · Script: LeaveThroughVortexL1 · Reward: the way to the second land · Repeatable: no

Sources: the land's control script and the quest's script (the original source text, which matches the shipped challenge.chl), the shared reminder script, the game's text table and the executable. openblack is judged on this tree: in a game that does not skip the creature guide the land's control script stops long before the storm (at Choose Your Creature's gate stones), and 3 of the 43 commands the quest needs only log "not implemented" in src/CHLApi.cpp.

Progress: 0/41 done, 11 partial — 13%
