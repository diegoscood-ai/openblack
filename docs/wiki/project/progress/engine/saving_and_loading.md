# Saving and loading

Saving the whole game to a slot and loading it back: the world with every object in it, the players and their creature,
the towns, the scripts, the camera, the weather and the temple. Saves live in the player's profile and are made from the
temple's save game room, by the quick save keys, by scripts, and by an autosave. openblack reads and writes the save files byte for byte
(`components/gamesave`, `apps/savetool`), and the debug bar's Load Island / Saved Games menu loads a slot into play:
the land, the scripts, the seeds, the clock, the trees, forests and big forests, the towns, the storage pits and their
piles, the footpaths, the citadels, the abodes, creches and graveyards, the towns' desire flags, the rivers, the banks
of mist, the climates and the snow lying on the land. Saving, writing a new game into a slot, is not ported yet.
