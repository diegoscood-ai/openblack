The Lost Brother

The second step of finding the creature-gate stones on the first land: a Norse woman's sick brother has wandered off from his sick-bed in a fever; if the player finds him and brings him back to her she hands over the ape gate stone kept in her house. The player can instead heal him, smash her house to get the stone, or kill either of them, and each way ends the story differently with its own alignment.

Land: 1 · Giver: the brother's sister, a Norse housewife, at her house, on the edge of the Norse village · Script: LostBrother · Reward: the ape gate stone (one of the three that open the creatures' gates), set on her doorstep · Repeatable: no

Sources: the land's challenge scripts (the original source text, checked against the PC game's compiled challenge.chl), the land's map script (Land1.txt) and the game's text table. openblack is judged on this tree: of the 85 commands the quest needs only 5 log "not implemented" in src/CHLApi.cpp (the record among them); the dialogue, advisor, scroll, villager-making, camera move, timer, effect and property commands work, and so do the pick-up, moveable, indestructible and fire flags, the camera cuts, fades, widescreen, music and game time. The land's control script stops before this quest is reached, in Choose Your Creature's gate-stone loop (see ../../scripts/land1_script.md), so none of it happens in game; rows are todo unless the notes say otherwise. The land as a whole is in ../land_1.md; the quest that starts it is Choose Your Creature.
