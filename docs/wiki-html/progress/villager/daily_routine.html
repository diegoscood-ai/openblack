Villager daily routine

What a villager does when nobody is directing it: it offers itself to what its town wants most, sees to its own hunger and tiredness, goes home at night and sleeps, and fills idle time by sitting out or wandering.

Progress: 28/36 done, 8 partial — 89%

How the original does it, in our wiki: Villagers: data, state machine and speed.
