Clans

Clans of players who play internet games together: a team can play for a clan, which then scores instead of its players, brings its own creature from game to game, and shows its logo as the team's symbol. The game asks the online servers for all of it; the clans themselves were run on the game's web site.

Codebase: bwgame-service (C:\projects\bwgame-service), the stand-in for the game's online servers and web site, not openblack. Every "done" below is done there; paths in the notes are relative to that repository. What the game itself does with clans in a match is in multiplayer_rules.md (openblack).
