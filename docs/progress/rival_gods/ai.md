# How a computer god decides

Every rival god, in the story and in skirmish, is driven by the same mind: a fixed tree of needs, from six broad aims
down to single hand actions, weighed each turn against what the god can see, how it feels about each other player and
what it has been told by the land's scripts. The scripts can tune the weights, pause the mind, take over its hand and
queue actions of their own.

**Progress: 15/66 done, 0 chosen, 22 partial, 0 inferred — 33%**

openblack has the mind's tree and each player's copy of it, the scripts' settings, the god's own hand with its steps
and its queue of actions, and its start on a land (`ComputerPlayerSystem`,
`src/ECS/Systems/Implementations/ComputerPlayerSystem.cpp`); the mind's own turn, which weighs the tree and runs what
it chooses, is written but not wired (`src/ECS/ComputerPlayerMind.h`; see
[../../../hand/computer-minds.md](../../../hand/computer-minds.md#pending)). The script functions that steer a god are
real except the camera's following of its hand (see [challenge_scripts.md](challenge_scripts.md)).
