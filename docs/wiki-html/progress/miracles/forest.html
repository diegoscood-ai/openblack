Forest

A seed miracle: thrown onto the land it plants eighteen magic trees in a spiral, which grow while the caster pays for them and wither away when the miracle ends. Sparkles, a light on the land and a flock of butterflies (or bats, for an evil god) follow, and the caster's camera takes a short flight round the new forest. There is no power-up.

Given by gold scroll: The Workshop.
