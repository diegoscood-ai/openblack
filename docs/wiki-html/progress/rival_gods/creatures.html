The computer gods' creatures

Each computer god has a creature, loaded from a mind file the game ships, set up by script and then steered by its god: fed, healed, taught, played with and sent to win, destroy or defend towns and to fight other creatures.

Progress: 2/47 done, 4 partial — 9%

See ../creature/ for how any creature thinks and acts, especially ../creature/decision_making.md, ../creature/fighting.md and ../creature/saves_and_files.md.
