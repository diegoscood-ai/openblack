Learning from feedback

The player teaches the creature with the hand: holding the action button on it puts the hand on its body, resting the hand there strokes it and sweeping the hand across it slaps it. When the hand lets go, the creature credits the reward or punishment to what it did lately and learns from it: how much to want the desire behind the action, which kinds of thing to do it to and use, and what it thinks of the action itself.
