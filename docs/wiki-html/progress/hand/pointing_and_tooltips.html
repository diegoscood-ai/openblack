Pointing and tooltips

What the hand tells the player about what it is over: the word, mouse button and arrows it shows by the cursor ("Pick Up", "Throw", "Rotate" …) and the numbers it shows over buildings, towns and the creature. How tooltips fade, their levels and their look belong to ../interface/; this file covers which tooltip the hand shows where.

Progress: 17/49 done, 4 partial — 39%

How the original does it, in our wiki: The hand and the interface.

How the hand picks its tooltip

A tooltip of a number fills the number into its text, rounded to a whole done help::tooltips builds the text with helptext::Format (the texts' %3.0f), the forced amount and the "/places" suffix
