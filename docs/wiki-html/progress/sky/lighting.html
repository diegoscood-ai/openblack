Lighting by time of day

The light the world is seen in: a palette of colours for the land through the day for each alignment, a dark colour, a warm colour and the moon's colour, which light the land, the models and the sea, and close the haze in at dusk.

Progress: 15/16 done, 1 partial — 97%

How the original does it, in our wiki: Day, night and weather in the original, World rendering: original versus openblack.
