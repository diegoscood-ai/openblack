Unused content

What was made for the game but never reaches the player: challenges whose scripts were never compiled into the shipped challenge file, scenes cut from shipped challenges, creature actions that do nothing, and data files nothing loads. "Unused" here was checked by comparing the script sources of the Mac release (the original sources, kept with the challenge compiler work in openblack's research folder) against the 514 scripts actually inside Scripts/Quests/challenge.chl. Unused puzzle kinds and the best-known cut games (cow bowling, whack-a-villager, the cup final and so on) are listed in ../story/minigames.md; they are not repeated here.
