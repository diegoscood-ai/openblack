Bink videos

The game ships five Bink videos: two start-up pictures, a pre-intro, the story's intro film, a strip of tip pictures for the loading screen and the film behind the creature's fall at the end of the game. This file is what each is for and when the game shows it; how they are decoded and drawn is in bink_playback.md. openblack plays the intro and the falling-spell film; the other three are only decoded, for the debug Video window.
