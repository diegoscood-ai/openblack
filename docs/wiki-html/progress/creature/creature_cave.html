The Creature Cave

The temple's room for the player's creature. The creature stands in it by a fire, and around the cave are scrolls telling of its attributes, its personality, the actions it has learnt and the miracles it knows, attack dummies hung with the belts it has won in fights and plinths with medals for its miracles. Clicking the creature opens the tattoo editor (creature_tattoos.md). The temple itself is in ../temple.

Progress: 17/31 done, 4 partial — 61%
