The wonder and tribal powers

Each tribe can build a wonder, a huge building raised from scaffolds and wood like any other. A finished, working wonder adds its tribe's power (from the tribe table) to its owner's power for that tribe. Each miracle's table says which tribes' powers make it stronger or cheaper; the Indian power also speeds up villagers and the Aztec power raises worship.
