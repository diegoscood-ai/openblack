Tornado

The tornado is the storm's second power-up: a storm whose centre grows a spinning funnel that wanders over the land, sucks up trees, people, animals and loose objects, carries them round and flings them away. The storm's clouds, rain and in-cloud lightning are described in storm.md.

Progress: 26/34 done, 3 partial — 81%

How the original does it, in our wiki: Vortexes and the tornado: objects swallowed, carried and flung, Miracles one by one.
