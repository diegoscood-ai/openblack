Skirmish opponents

The nameless computer gods of the skirmish lands (the land scripts in Scripts/Playgrounds) and of any temple no person plays in a network game. They use the same computer player mind as the story's rival gods, with creatures from the game's generic computer creature file. Everything about them comes from the land script: the skirmish box offers no opponent, difficulty or team settings.

Progress: 3/38 done, 14 partial — 26%

See ai.md for how they decide, creatures.md for their creatures and ../multiplayer/skirmish.md for the skirmish itself.
