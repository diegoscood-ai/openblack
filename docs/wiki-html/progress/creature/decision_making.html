Decision making

How a creature decides what to do next: it plans for its desires, picks the thing to act on and the action to take, turns the action into an agenda of small steps, carries them out, and copes when they fail. Scripts and the computer gods can also take control of a creature.

Progress: 18/63 done, 21 partial — 45%
