Fighting

Creatures fight each other in duels inside a circular arena. The player directs their own creature with the hand, clicking the opponent's body high, middle or low to strike, their own creature to block and the ground to step, or leaves it to fight by itself as it has learnt to. Nobody dies in a fight: the loser faints, is carried home and recovers.
