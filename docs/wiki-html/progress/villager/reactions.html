Villager reactions

Villagers react to what happens around them: they flee frightening miracles and watch pleasing ones, gather round the creature, fight fires, run from predators, point at flying objects, mourn the dead and are impressed, which gives their town belief. Each reaction has a reach, an urgency and a time from the reaction table.
