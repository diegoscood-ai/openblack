# The Lost Brother

The second step of finding the creature-gate stones on the first land: a Norse woman's sick brother has wandered off
from his sick-bed in a fever; if the player finds him and brings him back to her she hands over the ape gate stone kept
in her house. The player can instead heal him, smash her house to get the stone, or kill either of them, and each way
ends the story differently with its own alignment.

**Land:** 1 · **Giver:** the brother's sister, a Norse housewife, at her house, on the edge of the Norse village · **Script:** LostBrother · **Reward:** the ape gate stone (one of the three that open the creatures' gates), set on her doorstep · **Repeatable:** no

Sources: the land's challenge scripts (the original source text, checked against the PC game's compiled
`challenge.chl`), the land's map script (`Land1.txt`) and the game's text table. openblack is judged on this tree: none of
the commands the quest needs only logs "not implemented" in `src/CHLApi.cpp`; the dialogue, advisor, scroll,
villager-making, camera move, timer, effect, property and record commands work, and so do the pick-up, moveable,
indestructible and fire flags, the camera cuts, fades, widescreen, music and game time. A run on 2026-10-22 (Land 1,
SkipBox answer 0, deterministic fixed-step runs) reached the start of this quest in one launch from a new game: laying
the tiger gate stone in Choose Your Creature's platform sets `GateKey2Present` to 1 and starts `LostBrother` (see
[../../scripts/land1_script.md](../../scripts/land1_script.md)). The quest itself has not been played in a run yet;
rows are pending unless the notes say otherwise. The land as a whole is in [../land_1.md](../land_1.md); the quest that starts it is
[Choose Your Creature](choose_your_creature.md).
