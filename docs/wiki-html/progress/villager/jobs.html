Villager jobs

Every adult has a job from the villager tables: housewife, farmer, forester, fisherman, shepherd, leader or trader. Jobs gather food and wood for the town, and any adult can be sent to build, fetch or supply when the town wants it. A villager put to work by the hand becomes a disciple (see disciples.md).
