Food miracle

The horn of plenty: held at the side of the hand and poured with the right button, it showers grain that lands as food piles or goes straight into a storage pit. Its extreme version makes speed-up food. Grain on villagers' tables is in ../resources/.

Progress: 26/32 done, 4 partial — 88%

How the original does it, in our wiki: Miracles one by one.
