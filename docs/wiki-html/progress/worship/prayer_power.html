Prayer power

The magic the worshippers make. Each worship site stores it in a battery that its dancers fill, and the miracles draw on it to be summoned, kept going and powered up.

Progress: 12/22 done, 5 partial — 66%

How the original does it, in our wiki: Magic: the core of the miracles.
