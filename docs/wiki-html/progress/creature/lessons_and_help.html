Lessons and creature help

The game explains the creature to the player as it goes. When the creature learns something, moves to a new stage of growing up, is put under a spell, gets into trouble or fails at something, the good advisor comes to the edge of the screen and says so in a line or two of text. These messages wait their turn on a stack, aren't repeated too often, and can be switched off in the options. The general help system and advisors belong to ../interface/ and ../story/; this file covers what the creature has to say.
