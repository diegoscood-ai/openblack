Totem

Each village has a totem pole by its village centre. The player takes hold of the totem with the hand and slides it up or down to choose how many of the village's people worship rather than work. The hand locks onto the pole while the button is held. Worship itself is in ../worship/. Khazar teaches the totem on the second land, in Worship Site.
