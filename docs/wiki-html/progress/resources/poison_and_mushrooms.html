Poison and mushrooms

The world has three loose mushrooms: the plain mushroom, the magic mushroom and the toadstool. All three are food the hand can carry to a store, and only the toadstool is poisonous. Poison lives on food (a store's food, a pile, a pot, a handful) and on living things; it spreads to whoever takes from poisoned food, slowly wears villagers down, and is cured by the heal miracle. Scripts poison people and stores directly, as Lethys does in the Plague scroll on Land 2.
