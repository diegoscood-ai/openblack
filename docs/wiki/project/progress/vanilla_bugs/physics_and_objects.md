# Vanilla bugs: physics and objects

Bugs in the original game's physics engine and its handling of objects: bodies, collisions, breaking buildings, the
hand's grip and reward chests. Most come from the physics research (read from the executable), which is ported on the
physics branch; picking and land queries are in [engine.md](engine.md).

**Bugs: 11 (soft-locks and lost progress: 0)**

## Wrong outcomes

| Bug | What the player sees | Evidence | Found in | openblack |
|-----|----------------------|----------|----------|-----------|
| When a reward chest's miracle seed is one the player can already cast, the game looks for the first power-up level the player lacks, but its count of levels includes the plain miracle, so it reads one level past the last power-up | A storm chest given to a player who can cast storm and all three of its power-ups enables the magic numbered as storm's first power-up gesture and shows that magic's gesture on the land, instead of giving a one-shot globe; a seed with fewer power-ups reads nothing there and gives the globe as meant | The opening counts one level for the plain miracle and one per power-up gesture, then reads each level as the magic type after the plain one, so the last read lands on the first power-up gesture that follows the magic types in the seed's table row; read from the executable | [rewards.md](../story/rewards.md) | reproduced (`reward::MagicAtLevel`, test `RewardOpening.ThreePowerUpsReadOnIntoTheGestures`) |
