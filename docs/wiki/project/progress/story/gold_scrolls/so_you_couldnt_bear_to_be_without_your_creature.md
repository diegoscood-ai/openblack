# So You Couldn't Bear to Be Without Your Creature?

The third land's main gold quest, "free the creature": the player follows Lethys through the vortex and finds their
creature frozen in a prison of three stone pillars, each one fed by the prayers of a village. Winning a village sinks
its pillar; losing it raises the pillar again. When all three are down the creature is freed; later, once Lethys's last
town is in trouble, Lethys begs for his life, hands over the creed from Khazar's creature and opens the vortex to the
next land. The quest has no title of its own: its challenge-log entry is headed by Lethys's line "So you couldn't bear
to be without your Creature?", which is never actually spoken.

**Land:** 3 · **Giver:** the advisors, on arrival (no scroll; logged straight away) · **Script:** GetThroughVortexL2, FreeTheCreature · **Reward:** the creature back (leash and temple restored), the creed from Khazar's creature, and the exit vortex · **Repeatable:** no

The land as a whole is in [../land_3.md](../land_3.md), the land's map script in
[../../scripts/land3_script.md](../../scripts/land3_script.md), the vortex mechanics in [../portals.md](../portals.md).
The two attacks started part-way through have their own files: [the_wolves_are_possessed.md](the_wolves_are_possessed.md)
and [fire_fire_im_on_fire.md](fire_fire_im_on_fire.md); the exit is
[leave_through_the_vortex_land_3.md](leave_through_the_vortex_land_3.md). How the creature was taken at the end of Land 2:
[lethys_has_taken_our_creature.md](lethys_has_taken_our_creature.md).

## The quest run

Started directly, without the land's control script (its rows stay gate rows):

```sh
OB_SESSION=<session> sh tools/checks/story/quest_run.sh <label> <exe folder> \
  "SetupLand3/FreeTheCreature&/GetThroughVortexL2" quest.so_you_couldnt_bear 30000 \
  tools/checks/story/quests/so_you_couldnt_bear_to_be_without_your_creature.txt Land3.txt
```

The timeline `quest.so_you_couldnt_bear` (`src/Debug/TestbedQuestCouldntBear.cpp`) clicks the texts, lets the arrival
and the advisors' later remarks play, puts the camera over the prison, then wipes out the Japanese, Indian and
Egyptian villages one at a time, their buildings and their villagers, each waited on until its pillar has fallen and its
cut scene has come back. The people go too because the original rebuilds a village whose people survive: a deleted
building becomes one of its town's plans again (Abode::ToBeDeleted 0x402C60, MoveAbodeToPlannedAbodes 0x404520), a town
with no buildings stays while it has plans or people (Town::RemoveStructureFromTown 0x739A60), and its homeless
villagers ask for a plan to be built (Villager::CheckSatisfyAbodesDesire 0x758E30, Town::RequestANewAbode 0x73B330).
openblack does the same (`src/ECS/Abodes.cpp:477-481`, `src/ECS/Systems/Implementations/VillagerBuild.cpp:367-391`), so in run
qrp3_q5 each village read as standing again once a rebuilt building passed a tenth built. A village with
no building standing reads as the player's ([script-commands.md, PLAYER (21)](../../../../scripts/script-commands.md#properties)),
so this is the spec's shortcut for winning it. Giving a village to the player outright does not work: with no belief
of the player's in it, the town's next belief fold hands it back to neutral, in the original
([towns.md, the fold](../../../../beings/towns.md#the-fold-step-19), steps 4 to 6) as in openblack
(`src/ECS/Town/TownBelief.cpp:574-625`).

Run qrp3_q5 (2026-10-29, `refs/story/evidence/qrp3_q5.log`, 60000 frames): 52 of the 97 rows pass, 34 fail, 3 are gate rows, 1 a
shortcut and 7 have no spec line. The setup, the weather's pause, the prison's beams and freezes, Lethys's wolf being
made and its torments, the arrival film with every advisor line in order, the log entry and the pillars' falls and
rises with their cut scenes all play as the script says. The three pillars fall one after another (the Japanese at

knocked-down village reads as standing again (the Japanese one at turn 1535, line 116321; the Indian at turn 2216,
