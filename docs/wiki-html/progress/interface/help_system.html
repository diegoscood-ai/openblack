Help system

The help the game gives as it is played: the advisors' messages when something happens for the first time or goes wrong, the answers to the Help key, the creature's learning messages, alerts, banter and tips. Who the advisors are and how they look and fly is in ../story/; the words on screen are in on_screen_text.md.

Progress: 9/37 done, 10 partial — 38%

How the original does it, in our wiki: The hand and the interface.
