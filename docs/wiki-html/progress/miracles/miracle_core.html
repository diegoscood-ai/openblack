Miracle effects and reactions

The machinery every miracle shares once it is cast: its life from creation to removal, the effect it applies to the things it reaches, the deaths and damage it causes, how it moves alignment and belief, how people, animals and creatures react to it, the challenge scripts' miracle commands, computer gods casting miracles, and saving active miracles. Casting by hand is in casting_and_globes.md, what a miracle costs in prayer_cost.md, and creatures casting in creature_spells.md.

Progress: 46/94 done, 16 partial — 57%

How the original does it, in our wiki: Magic: the core of the miracles, Miracles one by one.
