Nemesis

The game's final enemy: a god who wants to be the only one. He kills Khazar on the second land, is behind Lethys and the curses that follow, and on the fifth land is the second player, whose four towns and temple the player must win before facing his creature.

Progress: 6/41 done, 8 partial — 24%

See ../story/land_5.md for the land's challenges and curses, khazar.md for Khazar's death, and ai.md, magic.md, towns_and_influence.md and creatures.md for how every computer god works.
