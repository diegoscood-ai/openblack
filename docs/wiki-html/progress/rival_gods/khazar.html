Khazar

The good rival god of the second land: the player's ally, who brought them through the vortex, gives them builders and scaffolds, comments on their deeds and is killed by Nemesis part-way through the land. He is the land's second player and is run by the computer player's mind, steered by the land's challenge scripts.

Progress: 5/46 done, 6 partial — 17%

See ../story/land_2.md for the land's challenges, and ai.md, magic.md, towns_and_influence.md and creatures.md for how every computer god works.
