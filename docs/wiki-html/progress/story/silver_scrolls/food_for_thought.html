The Nomads (Food for Thought)

A cut Land 2 challenge: when an Indian village's storage pit runs low on food, ten wandering homeless men arrive, and the player decides whether and how to feed them: food from the village's own store, food conjured by a miracle, poisoned food, or nothing. It was never compiled into the game; its title "The Nomads" exists in the text table but none of its lines do.

Land: 2 (written for its Indian village; never started) · Giver: an Indian farmer from the village · Script: FoodForThought · Reward: a large food reward from the sky, for feeding them with miracle food · Repeatable: no
