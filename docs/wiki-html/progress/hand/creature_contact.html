Creature contact

The hand's side of touching a creature: taking hold of it, stroking and slapping it, how the hand rests on and turns to the body, and what the creature is told when the hand lets go. What the creature learns from it is in ../creature/. Giving the creature something to hold or eat is in holding.md.

Progress: 24/46 done, 7 partial — 60%

How the original does it, in our wiki: The hand and the interface.
