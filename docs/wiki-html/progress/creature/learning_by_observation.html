Learning by observation

Besides the hand's rewards, the creature learns by watching. It picks up ordinary skills (building, fishing, dancing and so on) by watching villagers practise them, learns miracles by seeing them cast often enough, and copies what its god does: it notices a deed, does it itself, and may come to want what its god seemed to want. What it sees its god do also tells it what its god wants.

Progress: 14/80 done, 22 partial — 31%
