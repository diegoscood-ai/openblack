Disciples

A villager the player picks up and puts down on something becomes a disciple of that work and keeps at it for good: on a field a farmer, in a forest a forester, at the sea a fisherman, at a building site a builder, by another villager a breeder, in another town a missionary, at the workshop a craftsman, at the storage pit a trader, at the worship site a worshipper.

Progress: 1/21 done, 7 partial — 21%
