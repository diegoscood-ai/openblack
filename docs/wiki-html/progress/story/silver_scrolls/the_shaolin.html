The Shaolin

A silver scroll on the third land. A guru leaves his mountain temple to meditate in a secret place and asks the player not to follow him. The player has to follow him anyway, unseen, all the way down the mountain to his hidden spot. If the player succeeds, the guru later comes back, while the creature is being freed, to start a Wonder for the player's people.

Land: 3 · Giver: the guru (a Shaolin monk) at the mountain temple above the Japanese village; the scroll itself stands by the Japanese village · Script: Shaolin · Reward: a Wonder begun for the player about 20 minutes into the creature's rescue (only if this scroll was done first) · Repeatable: no (failed attempts restart until it is done)

The land as a whole is in ../land_3.md, the script program in ../../scripts/challenge_scripts.md.

Sources: the land's challenge script source (checked against the PC game's compiled challenge.chl), the game's text table (Scripts/InfoScript2.txt) and the executable. openblack is judged on this tree: of the 53 script functions the quest and the scripts it starts call, 3 only log "not implemented" in src/CHLApi.cpp, but Land 3's control script never runs (the land-loading command does nothing), so the guru never exists and every row below is todo unless the notes say otherwise. The reward scene calls 28 (none missing; BuildBuilding works).
