Creature physiology

A creature's body lives through time: it gets hungry, tired, thirsty, hot or cold, needs to poo, grows stronger from work and fatter from overeating, is healed by sleep and faints when exhausted, starved or out of life. Each species has its own rates, from the game's creature tables.
