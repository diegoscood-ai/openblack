Flocks

Two flock miracles are swept out of the hand. The flying flock makes doves for a good god or bats for an evil one; they fly off together, leave a trail and frighten creatures (bats). The ground flock makes a pack of wolves that run down a corridor, hunting and eating villagers in their way. Wild animals and livestock are in ../animal/.
