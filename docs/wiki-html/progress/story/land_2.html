Land 2

Khazar's land: the player arrives through the vortex to find the friendly god Khazar and the hostile Lethys. Khazar teaches worship and miracles, the player wins over the land's towns, and Lethys ends the land by stealing the creature.

Every silver scroll of the game, land by land, with scores: silver_scrolls.md.
