Lightning

A held miracle: while the player keeps casting, forked bolts leap from the hand to people, creatures, trees and buildings in a cone ahead of the camera, setting them alight. Two power-ups add a wider reach, more forks and more targets at once.

Given by silver scrolls: to a town by The Greedy Farmer (with its second level) and The Plague, and as a dispenser by the evil ending of The Pied Piper.

Progress: 36/43 done, 2 partial — 86%

How the original does it, in our wiki: Miracles one by one.
