How a computer god decides

Every rival god, in the story and in skirmish, is driven by the same mind: a fixed tree of needs, from six broad aims down to single hand actions, weighed each turn against what the god can see, how it feels about each other player and what it has been told by the land's scripts. The scripts can tune the weights, pause the mind, take over its hand and queue actions of their own.

Progress: 0/66 done, 0 partial — 0%

openblack has no computer god mind yet: a computer player is only a player entity (PlayerArchetype) and every script function that would steer one is a stub (see challenge_scripts.md).
