# Tutorial

How the game teaches itself: the opening of the first land, where the advisors and short hand demonstrations show the
camera, the hand and the first miracles. The separate practice island is in tutorial_island.md. Skipping the tutorial and the creature's training (patch 1.1)
is one row in land_1.md (the SkipBox row).

A cut early draft of the opening at the temple (rotating the camera, carrying wood and food to the builders, the kinds
of scroll) is in [silver_scrolls/see_the_citadel.md](../../not-in-the-original.md#storysilver_scrollssee_the_citadel).

Rows reachable only with a force-feedback device have the status `device`, counted apart and in no score (user, 2026-10-27; [progress/README.md](../README.md#status)).
