# raffclar's work in our tree

## Summary

openblack follows raffclar's tree (see [Following raffclar's tree](refactor-conventions.md#following-raffclars-tree)).
This page measures how much of his work is in ours, against his **current** `master`: every pull request merged into
it since upstream openblack's `a243c6b2`, and one more item for his few commits outside any pull request. Each is
**in**, **partial**, **pending** or **n/a**, with its evidence. The table is
[raffclar-status.tsv](raffclar-status.tsv); the [progress dashboard](dashboard.md#raffclars-work-in-our-tree) draws
the same numbers.

The older measure, the port rows of his fixed target build `0222f9f5`, is kept as history in the
[port audit](port-audit.md); its rows are part of the evidence below.

## The measure

*raffclar's work in our tree = (in + half of partial) / (all − n/a)*, counted in pull requests. Every pull request
counts once, whatever its size.

- **in**: ported (a commit of ours says "Ported from raffclar/openblack PR #N", or carries his commit as "cherry picked
  from commit"), or our tree has the same behaviour: the port rows that cover it are closed in the
  [port audit](port-audit.md), or the study of his lanes of 2026-10-17 found it already in ours. Where both trees did
  the same job and ours was kept, the choice is written in the porting conflicts list (`conflicts.md`), and it counts
  as in. Ported pull requests are not yet checked against v1.42; that audit comes after the import.
- **partial**: some of it is in: some of its port rows are closed and some open, some of its new files are in ours,
  or ours does the same job but the two have not been compared yet.
- **pending**: not in our tree yet, or not classified yet (the evidence says which). Nothing is counted as in without
  evidence.
- **n/a**: work that is deliberately not taken: his inspector and MCP adapter (parked by decision C-1), his own docs
  pages (ours are this wiki), CI and build tooling, formatting-only changes and reverts. His renderer switch to SDL3
  and slang would be n/a too (our renderer stays on bgfx, [Milestones](milestones.md#staying-on-bgfx)), but his
  `master` has not taken it.

How each was decided: a pull request before his target build is judged by the port rows that cite it or name its
files; with no such row, by its new files (in our tree at the same path, or done elsewhere in ours by the direct-port
audit of 2026-10-15). A later one is judged by our port commits and the 2026-10-17 study of his lanes (its gain table is
[raffclar_uplift.tsv](raffclar_uplift.tsv)); those merged
after that study were read from their diffs. `docs/tools/progress_stats.py --raffclar <clone>` adds every new pull
request of his master as pending, to be classified.
