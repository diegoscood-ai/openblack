On-screen text

The words the game writes over the screen: what the advisors and characters say, text that land scripts put up, titles, and the game's text database and fonts that all of it comes from. The script functions themselves are listed in ../story/.

Progress: 12/24 done, 6 partial — 62%

How the original does it, in our wiki: The hand and the interface.
