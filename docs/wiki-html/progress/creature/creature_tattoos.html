Creature tattoos

The player can tattoo their creature with up to eight symbols, one on each of up to eight places on its body, each in a colour of their choosing. Tattoos are put on and taken off in a tattoo editor, reached by clicking the creature in the temple's Creature Cave (or from the player's profile in the main menu), and are painted into the creature's skin, where they stay as it grows, changes shape and turns evil or good.

How tattoos are drawn
