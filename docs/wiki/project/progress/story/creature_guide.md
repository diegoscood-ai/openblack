# Creature Guide

The gold-scroll story of the first land's second half, shown under the scroll title "The Creature's Learning": a huge
creature, once Nemesis's, wanders a valley, asks to meet the player's creature, befriends it, teaches the player to
impress the Aztec village with a food miracle (and the creature to dance), teaches the creature to fight, and is killed
by Nemesis's storm on a mountain top as it is about to tell the secret of the Creed. The storm then turns on the
player's village and, a couple of minutes later, the exit vortex shows itself. The land as a whole is in
[land_1.md](./land_1.md), the vortex in [portals.md](./portals.md), the script program in
[../scripts/challenge_scripts.md](../scripts/challenge_scripts.md).

Sources: the land's challenge scripts (the original source text, checked line by line against the PC game's compiled
`challenge.chl`), the game's text table and the executable. openblack's state is judged on this tree: every script function
the guide's scripts and the scripts they start need is real, a constant or partial in `src/CHLApi.cpp` (the desires, the
fight natives and the challenge record are real), and a script can make the guide now (`CreateScriptObject`).
`CREATURE_DO_ACTION` (`CreatureDoAction` in `src/CHLApi.cpp`) forces a row of the creature's actions as a plan by its name, and the five
rows these scripts use that once had no executor have one now in `src/Creature/CreaturePlanActions.cpp` (2026-10-18 and
2026-10-20): 17 SleepAtHome, 64 HoldObject, 91 WaveAtObject, 163 PointAtObject and 261 DeadForever (tests
`CreatureActionAgendas.SleepingAtHomeWalksHomeFacesDownTheSlopeAndSleepsUntilRested`, `WavingGoesNearAndFacesTheOther`,
`FrightenedPuttingDownAndLyingDead`, `HoldingAThingUpPicksItUpTurnsToTheCameraPlaysAndWatches`,
`CreatureScriptAgendas.PointingAtAThingOrTheCamera`); none has been seen on the guide yet. The land's control script has not yet been run that far without skipping (a run of
2026-10-22 reached Choose Your Creature's first gate stone; the guide is skipped when the creature training is), so the
guide has not yet appeared in a run; every row below is pending unless the notes say otherwise. The food lesson has a quest
run of its own, not yet run: quest=SetupLand1/GuideImpressTownLesson on Land1.txt, timeline quest.guide_impress_town
(`src/Debug/TestbedQuestGuideImpressTown.cpp`), its scroll pressed on and its texts clicked.
