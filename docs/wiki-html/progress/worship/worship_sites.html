Worship sites

Each tribe a player wins over gets a worship site beside the player's temple, where that tribe's villagers dance to make prayer power. The site holds the tribe's miracle icons, a food store for the dancers and an altar. Artefacts put down at a site gain worth there: see ../town/artefacts.md.
