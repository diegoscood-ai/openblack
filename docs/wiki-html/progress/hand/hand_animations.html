Hand animations

Every animation of the god hand and when the game plays it. The animations are in the hand's animation file, named by the hand spec: each cycle is followed by two lean ranges (sideways, and back and forth) that the trailing cursor blends in. Some slots in the spec are spare and never used.

Progress: 19/35 done, 5 partial — 61%

How the original does it, in our wiki: The hand and the interface.
