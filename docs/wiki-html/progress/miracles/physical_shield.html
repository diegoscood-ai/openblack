Physical shield

A solid dome the player draws with a circle gesture. It rises out of the land, spins down and bobs, and stops thrown rocks and other physical objects; each blow drains the caster's prayer power. Miracles pass through it.

Given by gold scroll: Khazar's Shield Challenge.

Progress: 23/28 done, 2 partial — 86%

How the original does it, in our wiki: Miracles one by one.
