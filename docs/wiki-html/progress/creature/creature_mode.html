Creature Mode and the status panel

The camera can lock onto a creature and follow it about while the screen shows how damaged, hungry and tired it is. Hovering the hand over any creature brings up the same panel, with the reward the hand has given it so far. General camera controls are in ../camera.

Progress: 16/31 done, 11 partial — 69%

How the original does it, in our wiki: The creature: groundwork, random streams and what is unknown.
