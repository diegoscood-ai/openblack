Villagers in the hand and under script

The player can pick villagers up, carry, drop and throw them, and the challenge scripts can take villagers over to walk them, play animations and gather crowds. These rows cover the villager's side; the hand and physics own the rest.

Progress: 8/16 done, 6 partial — 69%
