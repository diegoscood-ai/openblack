Developer jokes

Jokes, puns, nods to Lionhead and its people, and developers' messages to each other, in the game's text and inside the program. The game's text is in Scripts/InfoScript2.txt (UTF-16); the in-game credits are owned by ../temple/library_room.md; the named villagers taken from the staff list by ../villager/special_villagers.md.
