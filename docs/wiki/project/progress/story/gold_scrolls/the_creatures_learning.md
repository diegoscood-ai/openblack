# The Creature's Learning

The first half of the first land's creature story: once the creature is chosen, Sable the creature trainer comes out of
the temple four times to teach the player to look after it — its new home and stroking, feeding it, slapping it, the
Leash of Learning (pulling it and making it pick up a cow) and tying the leash to a tree, where she also shows the
Leashes of Aggression and Compassion before saying farewell. The same gold scroll then carries on with the big creature
that wanders the valley; that half is in [creature_guide.md](../creature_guide.md).

**Land:** 1 · **Giver:** Sable the creature trainer, at the creature pen by the temple · **Script:** CreatureDevelopment (CreatureDevSeeHome, CreatureDevLearnToEat, CreatureDevPunishment, CreatureDevLeashIntro, CreatureDevLeashAttachToHouse) · **Reward:** none (the lessons hand over the three leashes) · **Repeatable:** no

Sources: the lessons' script source (checked against the shipped compiled program), the land's control script and the
game's text table. openblack is judged on this tree: a run on 2026-10-22 (Land 1, SkipBox answer 0) reached Choose Your Creature's
first gate stone, so the lessons are not yet reached without skipping (see
[../../scripts/land1_script.md](../../scripts/land1_script.md)). A quest run that starts them from the glade's choice
plays the first three lessons to their ends and the leash lesson to its cow step, where it stops (2026-10-28; see
[The quest run](#the-quest-run) and [Pending](#pending)). No command the lessons use only logs "not implemented" in
`src/CHLApi.cpp`; the creature's actions are forced by their row's name (`CREATURE_DO_ACTION`, `CreatureDoAction` in `src/CHLApi.cpp`),
and of the rows used, 168 PointAtCamera, 82 LookAtCamera, 161 BeIdle, 15 Hurl and 17 SleepAtHome have executors
(`src/Creature/CreaturePlanActions.cpp`). The desires, the held object, the slap and stroke
reads, the creature's energy and exhaustion and the challenge log (`SNAPSHOT`, `UPDATE_SNAPSHOT`) are real. The rest work: making the trainer, the scroll, the dialogue, the camera moves, the leash questions and
the leash-granting developer functions, drawing the leash, setting the game time, music, fades and the
moveable/pick-up/indestructible flags. Rows are pending unless the notes say otherwise.

## The quest run

The lessons are played on their own, without the land's control script, by a quest run
([../../../scenarios.md](../../../scenarios.md#playing-a-timeline-on-a-land), `tools/checks/story/README.md`):

```sh
OB_SESSION=<session> sh tools/checks/story/quest_run.sh <label> <exe folder> "SetupLand1/CreaturesInGlade" \
    quest.glade_choice/quest.the_creatures_learning 45000 tools/checks/story/quests/the_creatures_learning.txt
```

- The land entry starts `SetupLand1`, then `CreaturesInGlade` (the glade's scene). The timeline `quest.glade_choice`
  (`src/Debug/TestbedScenarioStory.cpp`) first has the temple built, as the story has it by then and as the control
  script's skip branch builds it (the script, `refs/chl/LandControl1.txt:67892`..`67914`): a quest run has only the
  temple's plan, and the Leash of Learning hangs on a post of the temple's heart. It waits for the scene's last line
  and presses on the ape twice to choose it.
- The timeline `quest.the_creatures_learning` (same file) starts `CreatureDevelopmentSet`, which runs the five lessons
  in the control script's order, and plays them with the player's inputs. Each step is marked in the timeline:
  `[real]` pointer input (the holds, strokes, slaps, scroll presses, the press on the leash post, the taps on the
  ground and on the cow), `[camera]`, `[hand]` (the giving, `HandGive`, and the two leashings, `HandTapLeash`) and
  `[shortcut]` (the food put into the hand, `HandTake`; its row is marked shortcut in the spec).
- `tools/checks/story/quests/the_creatures_learning.txt` holds the rows' patterns, each from the script's source;
  `quest_check.py` gives each row PASS, FAIL, GATE (a start-gate row, checked from a natural run only), SHORTCUT or
  NOSPEC.

**The run of 2026-10-28** (Land 1, 45000 frames): 76 rows pass, 39 fail, 9 gate, 1 shortcut and 11 have no pattern,
of 136.
- The first three lessons run to their ends: the hold and the strokes (3858, then the trainer's leaving line 3860),
  the meal (3867, 3866) and, after the creature throws the trainer, the slap (4043, 4046).
- The leash lesson: the Leash of Learning is picked by a press on its post (`GET_OBJECT_LEASH_TYPE` gives 2 at turn
  4656), the creature is leashed (`IS_LEASHED` true at turn 4715), two taps on the ground by the tree bring it within
  20 of the tree with the trainer there (the step's end at turn 5044), and the cow step begins (the creature wanting
  only to look at things, turn 5272).
- **It stops at the cow step.** The hand is over the cow and the Action button is clicked on it (the press reads
  "over object" the cow); the lesson then waits for the creature to hold the cow (the script,
  `refs/chl/CreatureDevelopment.txt:8851`..`8856`). At the run's end, turn 7201, `GET_OBJECT_HELD` of the creature
  still gives nothing and the creature stands 8.06 from the cow's point (`GET_DISTANCE`, turn 7201). The tying lesson
  never starts, so every row from the cow's pick-up on fails.

**Every stop found so far was the testbed's input, not a difference from the original.** Each was fixed in the
timeline, and the game was left as it is:
- The gift was given too far from the creature: a gift is called off as soon as the hand is more than 15 times the
  creature's size from it ([../../creature/feeding_and_thrown_things.md](../../creature/feeding_and_thrown_things.md),
  "Moving the hand away"). The timeline moves the hand to the creature before it gives.
- The trainer's last line of the punishment cut-scene (4040) was clicked while she still flew from the throw. The
  script then sends her back to her spot, and a move order on a thing in flight is dropped, in the original as in
  openblack: `MOVE_GAME_THING` acts on a villager only while it is in the map and not drowning (bw1-decomp
  `src/Black/ScriptFunctions.cpp:647`; openblack `villager_script::MoveTo`), and a thrown villager is out of the map
  for its flight. She came to rest one turn after the order and never walked. The timeline lets 4040 be read for four
  seconds.
- The temple was missing in the quest run, so there were no leash posts: `GET_OBJECT_LEASH_TYPE` gave 0, its answer
  with no temple, not -1 (nothing picked) ([../../../../creature/temple-leash-posts.md](../../../../creature/temple-leash-posts.md#the-draw)).
  The glade's timeline builds the temple (above).
- Presses made while a cut-scene still held the camera were lost, and the camera set then was undone by the
  cut-scene's own last move. The timeline waits for the lesson's reminder script, or its first nag, which start as the
  cut-scene gives the camera back.
- The slap or stroke outcome depended on the view: sweeps across the creature lengthwise read as a stroke. The
  timeline sweeps from side to side across the screen and waits for the trainer going back, whichever was felt.
- The leash taps landed on the post's rope: from the leash cut-scene's last view the line to the ground by the tree
  runs through the learning post, so the two taps unpicked and picked the leash again and ordered nothing. The timeline
  taps from east of the post once the camera is back ([../../../../creature/leash.md](../../../../creature/leash.md#tying-the-leash-with-the-hand)).
