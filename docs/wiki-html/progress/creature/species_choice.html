Species and choosing a creature

The player's creature is one of the game's animal species. At the start of the first land the player chooses between three young creatures; later, silver scroll challenges offer other species (../story/silver_scrolls/creature_swaps.md), which the player can swap to, keeping everything their creature has learnt. Each species looks, moves, sounds and grows its own way and has its own appetite, strength and knack for learning miracles.
