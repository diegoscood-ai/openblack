Dance scripts

Villagers dance at worship sites, around totems and artefacts, when buildings are finished, at the town's celebrations and with the creature. Each dance is a choreography file in Scripts/Dance/ (dancers' steps and actions key-framed to the beat), chosen through the dance table in info.dat. openblack reads the table (InfoConstants, its dance entries) but has no dances: worshippers only walk to a ring round the worship site and pray there (VillagerWorship.cpp), and the villagers' dance states are listed as to-do in LivingActionSystem.cpp. What dancing does for worship is the worship domain's (../worship/).
